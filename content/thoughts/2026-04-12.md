Loved the friendship between Grace and Rocky in Project Hail Mary. 

Two scientists from two different planets, different civilisations - sent on the same mission to save their planet. 

The most interesting part was how Grace figured out a way to communicate with Rocky. Started with the universal language: math. He had rocky spell out then numbers in his voice and then built a speech-to-text / text-to-speech interface with around 250 words vocabulary. He added words to the vocab as they went on. 

Loved how Rocky suggested to sacrifice half his fuel and delay going to his planet Erid by 6 years..only so he could send Grace back to Earth. He even risked his own life to save Grace when they came back from Tau seti. 

One of the best movies of all time, IMO.
