title: "Batch normalization works for the wrong reasons"
---

Batch normalization ([Ioffe & Szegedy, 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03167)) was justified as reducing "internal covariate shift." [Santurkar et al. at MIT (2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.11604) tested this directly and found BN doesn't reduce covariate shift. In some cases it increases it. The real reason it works: it smooths the loss landscape, making gradients more predictable so you can use higher learning rates. One of deep learning's most used techniques, adopted for years on an incorrect theory.
