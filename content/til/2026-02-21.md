title: "SkillsBench: Smaller Models with Skills Beat Larger Models"
---

- **Main finding**: Haiku + Skills can match Opus without Skills in task performance
- **Curated Skills boost**: +16.2 percentage points improvement with hand-crafted Skills
- **Self-generated Skills**: No measurable benefit—quality matters far more than quantity
- **Focused Skills > Comprehensive Docs**: Targeted, task-specific Skills outperform broad documentation
- **Implication**: Skills are a force multiplier, not just a nice-to-have. They can compress model capability gaps
- **Paper**: [SkillsBench: Benchmarking Skill-Augmented Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12670) (arxiv:2602.12670, Feb 13, 2026)

**Takeaway**: Skill engineering is as valuable as model scaling—sometimes more so.
